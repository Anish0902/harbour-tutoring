Questions parents ask

Can't see your question? Send us a message and we'll answer it.

Do you follow the NSW syllabus?

Yes. Group sessions follow a term plan matched to the NSW syllabus for each year group.

Can my child switch from group to private sessions?

Yes, at any time. Just let us know and we'll adjust the next invoice.

What happens if we miss a session?

Cancel with 24 hours' notice and the session is credited to the following week.

Do tutors have a Working With Children Check?

Every tutor holds a current NSW Working With Children Check before they start.
