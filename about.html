About Harbour Tutoring

A small tutoring business started in 2024 by two former high-school teachers.

Why we started

After years in the classroom, we saw the same thing again and again: students who fell behind on one topic kept falling further behind, because a class of thirty can't stop and wait. Harbour Tutoring exists to give students that pause.

We keep groups tiny, we follow the NSW syllabus closely, and we tell parents honestly how things are going.
